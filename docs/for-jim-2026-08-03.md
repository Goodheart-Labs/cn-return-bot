# For Jim — what Nathan + Claude found this week, and proposals (2026-08-03)

Compressed from a week of analysis; full evidence in `docs/things-we-know-2026-07-30.md` (flat fact list, confidence-tagged) and `docs/improvement-menu-2026-07-25.md` (backtest detail). Numbers below are from fresh Supabase pulls unless dated otherwise.

## The big one: your velocity floor doubled our coverage

Rated-at-all jumped **9.3% → 17.2%** in exactly the week the floors landed (Jul 20–21), while quality-of-rated barely moved — so helpfuls AND unhelpfuls both rose, net rate went +5.2% → +8.9%, and the cap recovery followed. Before/after evidence, not a controlled test, and two confounds landed the same week (Trump topic live Jul 18–19, reserve raised 2→5), but no other pipeline change fits the timing.

This also reframes your `velocity_helpful` figure: yes, ~half of *historical* helpful notes came from below 30k/hr — but those came from the regime where notes mostly went unseen. The floor traded low-velocity breadth for visibility and the trade is clearly winning so far. With a rated-at-all term in the model, the optimal floor may sit at or above 30k, not below.

## Other findings you'd want

- **Eval curve** (X's evaluate_note score, our only live quality signal): monotone — <0 ≈ +2% net, [0,0.5) ≈ +5%, [0.5,1) ≈ +7%, ≥1.0 ≈ +13–15% (historically 12H/0NH in the top band). Blind spot: flat on rated-at-all — eval is a quality signal, velocity is the coverage signal. They're complements.
- **Stale submissions:** since the floor, **11.9% of submissions went out >24h after the tweet** (queue-aged candidates — the floor screens at fetch and never re-checks). They earn half a fresh slot: 7.9% rated / +3.4% net vs 16.1% / +7.3%. → **PR #334** adds an at-submit cutoff (24h regular / 48h curated-topic), same partition pattern as your floor.
- **Time-travel A/B (#323, live):** prompts now (50/50) tell searcher+writer that a claim true-at-posting-but-outdated-by-events isn't a correctable error. Backtested on 398 rated notes: −11–15% of NH at ~1% H cost. Read via abstention-rate guard + Nathan's breaking-news tag rate per arm, ~mid-Aug.
- **Since 3368f41 (submit in pipeline order), nothing at submit time looks at eval.** Deliberate trade for coverage, and defensible — but it means the only quality control left is the gate at 0, and sub-0.5 notes run break-even at best while feeding NH_5 risk.
- **Supply ceiling ahead:** cap is climbing toward 40+; `MAX_POSTS_CAP = 20` × ~24 runs × ~7% conversion supplies only ~30–35 notes/day. The funnel becomes the binding constraint before X does.
- **Cap ladder context:** we're at ~8–9% net (allows ~40–45/day); crossing 10% unlocks the 50–200 band; 500/day is the hard wall at ≥20%.

## Proposals (Nathan-endorsed direction; your judgment on mechanics)

1. **Raise `MAX_POSTS_CAP` 20 → 30** (Nathan wants this; ~+$15/day, only bites on budget-rich runs since `computeMaxPosts` already sizes to remaining budget). One open question for you: Action runtime headroom at 30.
2. **Flat eval floor at 0.5 at submit** — the companion that keeps the extra depth from converting to break-even submissions on slot-rich days. One partition, reversible, same shape as #334.
3. **The real piece: a joint admission fit** — expected contribution per candidate from tier × velocity × eval, *with a rated-at-all term* (your velocity data is the foundation; the tier gradient — small +9.0% > large +6.7% > xl +4.0% > xxl −3.5% all-time — carries signal velocity doesn't). Replaces three uncoordinated filters with one measured bar. We'd want to build this with you.

Wants a weekly number; blocked mainly on view-data freshness (manual scrape, ~8wk stale).
