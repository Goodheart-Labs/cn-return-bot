# Time Sensitive: Stop One of the Biggest Threats for Animal Welfare

By Becca Rogers (May 4) — https://forum.effectivealtruism.org/posts/vsYphZaBcXpmtNizp/time-sensitive-stop-one-of-biggest-threats-for-animal

## What the bill is

The **"Save Our Bacon" (SOB) Act** appears as **Section 12006** of the Farm Bill, which **passed the House on April 30**. It would **eliminate states' ability to set their own farm-animal welfare standards** and would **void California's Proposition 12 and Massachusetts' Question 3**.

- Prop 12 / Question 3 set minimum space requirements and target practices like **gestation crates** (for sows) and **veal crates** (for calves).
- **Egg-laying hens are exempted** from the SOB Act's rollback in the relevant text; the rollback centers on pigs and calves.

## Scale of impact

- Rethink Priorities estimates roughly **488,000 pigs and ~45,000 calves per year** would be affected by losing these protections.
- About **4.5 million pigs** are still kept in gestation crates in the US.

## Politics and players

- **Sen. John Boozman (R-AR)** is expected to introduce a Senate version.
- **Smithfield** produces ~**25% of US pork** (owned by the Chinese **WH Group**); **JBS** produces ~**14%** (Brazilian-owned).
- **32 Senate Democrats** signed a letter opposing the provision.
- A **2022 poll** found support for Prop 12-style laws at **84% of Democrats, 79% of Independents, and 76% of Republicans** — broad bipartisan public support.

## Takeaway

The SOB Act / Section 12006 would federally preempt state farm-animal welfare laws (notably Prop 12 and Question 3), affecting hundreds of thousands of pigs and calves annually. Posts about it should be checked against these specifics: which animals are covered (pigs/calves, not egg hens), the House passage date, and the state laws it would void.
