# Brief: AI energy and carbon footprint

Source: https://blog.andymasley.com/p/a-cheat-sheet-for-conversations-about

A post is misleading on this topic if it claims that an individual's ChatGPT/LLM use is bad for the climate, or wildly overstates per-prompt energy/carbon.

Key debunking facts:
- A typical prompt uses ~0.3 Wh — about a 2009 Google search — and emits ~0.28 g CO2 (~1/150,000th of an American's daily emissions).
- Going vegan for a year saves the emissions of ~1,000,000 prompts; diet/transport choices dwarf prompt use.
- Data centers were ~0.5% of global emissions in 2024; all AI data centers ~43 Mt CO2 (~0.11% of world emissions).
- Modern hyperscale PUE ~1.1 (only ~10% overhead). IEA estimates AI could enable ~1,400 Mt CO2 reductions by 2035.

A post is NOT misleading for discussing aggregate data-center grid buildout as an infrastructure topic, or for stating accurate figures.
