# Brief: OpenAI's Department of Defense deal

Source: https://www.techpolicy.press/five-unresolved-issues-in-openais-deal-with-the-department-of-defense/

A post is misleading on this topic if it misstates OpenAI's DoD deal — e.g. claims OpenAI is building autonomous "killer robots," or conversely that the deal poses no concerns at all.

Key facts (the truth is nuanced):
- OpenAI announced a DoD deal with three public red lines: no mass domestic surveillance, no directing autonomous weapons, no high-stakes automated decisions.
- In Feb 2026 the DoD designated Anthropic a "supply chain risk."
- The article's point is that key terms are undefined or loophole-prone: "mass domestic surveillance" isn't tied to EO 12333/FISA; "intentional"/"deliberate" qualifiers create gaps; tracking-vs-surveillance is ambiguous; legal rationales may avoid public review (cf. Carpenter 2018, OLC torture memos); intelligence-agency exclusion may not be durable.

A post is NOT misleading for accurately describing the red lines or raising the unresolved-oversight concerns. Flag posts that assert false specifics (e.g. "OpenAI is directing autonomous weapons") as correctable.
