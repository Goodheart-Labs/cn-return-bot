# Brief: Save Our Bacon Act / farm animal welfare

Source: https://forum.effectivealtruism.org/posts/vsYphZaBcXpmtNizp/time-sensitive-stop-one-of-biggest-threats-for-animal

A post is misleading on this topic if it misstates what the "Save Our Bacon" (SOB) Act / Section 12006 of the Farm Bill does, or which animals/laws it affects.

Key facts:
- The SOB Act = Section 12006 of the Farm Bill, which passed the House on April 30. It would eliminate states' ability to set farm-animal welfare standards and void California's Prop 12 and Massachusetts' Question 3.
- It targets gestation crates (sows) and veal crates (calves); egg-laying hens are exempted in the relevant text.
- Rethink Priorities estimates ~488,000 pigs and ~45,000 calves/yr affected; ~4.5M pigs are still in gestation crates.
- Sen. Boozman (R-AR) expected to introduce a Senate version. 32 Senate Democrats signed an opposition letter. A 2022 poll found 84% D / 79% I / 76% R support for Prop 12-style laws.

A post is NOT misleading for accurately describing the bill or expressing an opinion on it. Flag posts that get the specifics wrong (e.g. claim it protects animals, or that it covers egg hens).
