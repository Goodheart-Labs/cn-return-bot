# Brief: Effective Altruism's achievements

Source: https://willmacaskill.substack.com/p/300000-lives-100-million-hens-and

A post is misleading on this topic if it claims Effective Altruism (EA) has achieved nothing / accomplished nothing / is useless / all talk — a blanket dismissal of the movement's measurable track record.

Key debunking facts (from Will MacAskill, 2025):
- Global health: since 2015, over $2 billion raised for the most effective global-health charities from 100,000+ donors, resulting in over 300,000 lives saved (mainly children).
- Animal welfare: EA-funded corporate cage-free campaigns helped move US cage-free hens from 10% (2012) to 46% (2025) — roughly 100 million hens per year spared from battery-cage confinement; the largest impact in decades of animal-welfare activism.
- Pandemic preparedness & AI: EA seeded the now-thriving fields of biosecurity, AI safety, and AI governance, which were treated as fringe concerns a decade ago.
- The movement is growing, not dying: effective giving rose ~40% in the last year (nearing $2 billion/year — top-5-foundation scale); 1,000+ new Giving What We Can pledges (+24% YoY); CEA metrics growing 10–30%/year.

A post is NOT misleading for critiquing specific EA failures (e.g. the FTX/SBF collapse), debating cause prioritization, or disagreeing with EA philosophy on normative grounds — only the blanket "EA has accomplished nothing" factual claim is correctable here.
