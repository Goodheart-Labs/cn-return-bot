# Brief: AI water use

Source: https://blog.andymasley.com/p/the-ai-water-issue-is-fake

A post is misleading on this topic if it claims that using AI / ChatGPT meaningfully harms water supplies, that a prompt or email "uses a bottle of water," or that AI data centers are draining local water.

Key debunking facts:
- AI/data center onsite water use is ~0.04% of US freshwater; per-prompt water is ~2 mL (incl. offsite electricity) or ~0.3 mL onsite — a fraction of a teaspoon.
- The viral "bottle of water per email/prompt" figure is inflated ~50–250x; it bundles offsite power-plant water and worst cases as if typical.
- The average American's daily water footprint (~422 gal) equals ~800,000 prompts.
- Famous cases are misattributed: Georgia water issues were construction sediment, not operations; the Amazon Oregon nitrate contamination came from farms (Amazon was 1 of 17 defendants).

A post is NOT misleading just for discussing data-center water as a local infrastructure topic, or stating accurate figures.
