# Brief: Trump's 2020 "stolen election" claims

Source: https://www.cbsnews.com/news/trump-election-primetime-speech-declassified-documents-revisits-disputed-claims/

A post is misleading on this topic if it asserts as fact that the 2020 (or 2022/2024) US election **outcome was altered** — votes flipped, voting machines hacked, the result "rigged"/"stolen," noncitizen voting decided it, or foreign interference (e.g. China) changed the result — including claims that the 16 July 2026 declassified documents *prove* any of this.

Key debunking facts:
- No evidence — including the government's own newly declassified documents — shows any vote was changed or any outcome altered. Trump task-force appointee John Solomon conceded the IC has "zero evidence that a foreign power flipped a vote in 2020, '22 or '24"; the White House conceded the docs don't allege switched votes or hacked machines.
- China obtained (largely **public/purchasable**) voter-registration data; possessing rolls ≠ changing votes. The 2021 ICA found China stayed neutral / did not try to change the 2020 outcome.
- Noncitizen-voter roll figures (~278k) are inflated by false positives/dead/eligible voters; documented noncitizen *voting* is minuscule (Georgia 2024: 20 of 8.2M; Brennan: 30 of 23.5M).
- Smartmatic machines aren't used in the US; tabulators are offline, paper-backed, and audited. Georgia counted every 2020 ballot 3 times (machine, full hand audit on human-readable text, Trump-requested recount) — all affirmed Biden by 11,779.

A post is NOT misleading if it: states these accurate facts (China got public voter data; fraudulent Michigan *applications* were caught; adversaries have *capability*; docs were declassified), argues election-security/voter-roll *policy* as opinion, discusses foreign influence operations as distinct from vote-changing, or merely reports on the speech. Correct false factual claims of a changed outcome — never opinions or policy views.
