# Brief: Data center land use

Source: https://blog.andymasley.com/p/data-center-land-use-issues-are-fake

A post is misleading on this topic if it claims data centers are devouring American farmland or that their land footprint is a major threat to agriculture.

Key debunking facts:
- All US data centers projected at ~1,400 sq mi by 2028 (~0.3% of prime farmland); actual building footprint only ~25 sq mi (~0.005%).
- The Conservation Reserve Program pays farmers ~$1.85B/yr to keep ~40,000 sq mi idle — ~28x the data-center footprint.
- US farmland shrank ~108,000 sq mi (Colorado-sized) from 2000–2024 — ~77x the data-center footprint; data centers are not the driver.
- Ethanol corn covers ~1.5% of the contiguous US (far more land than data centers) and is ~24% dirtier than gasoline.
- Loudoun County VA: data centers on ~3% of land generate ~38% of county revenue.

A post is NOT misleading for raising legitimate local zoning, grid, or water concerns, or for stating accurate figures.
