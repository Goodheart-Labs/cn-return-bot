Privacy Policy

Common Notes (commonnotes.net) · Last updated 10 July 2026

What we collect

  • Reading: nothing personal — no account needed, no tracking cookies.
  • Signing in with email: your email address, used only to send login links.
  • Signing in with X: your X handle, display name, and the email X shares with us.
  • Contributing: your votes, suggested improvements, and draft notes, linked to your account. Draft notes display your handle or display name publicly.

Where it lives

Account data and contributions are stored with Supabase (our database provider). Login emails are delivered via Resend. The site is served through Cloudflare. We do not sell your data or use third-party advertising or analytics trackers.

Cookies

Only what sign-in requires: a session token in your browser's local storage. No advertising or cross-site tracking.

Your choices

  • You can delete your own draft notes in the interface.
  • To delete your account and its data, email us and we'll do it.
