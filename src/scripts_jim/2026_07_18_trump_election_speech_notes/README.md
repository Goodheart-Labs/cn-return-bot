# Community Notes for Trump's July 16, 2026 election-security speech

**Goal:** if a Republican tweets one of Trump's speech claims, have a ready gold-standard
Community Note that adds context — sourced so a *Republican rater* marks it "helpful."

**Why the source constraint is non-negotiable.** On X a note only displays if it earns
"helpful" ratings from people who normally rate differently (the bridging algorithm). A note
sourced to CBS, Brookings, the Brennan Center, or David Becker/CEIR is dead on arrival — the
Republican raters it needs will score it "unhelpful/biased." So every rebuttal below is anchored
in one of: **Trump's own appointees, Republican secretaries of state, conservative institutions
(Heritage), or Republican/Trump-appointed judges.** Using in-group sources is not a style choice
here; it's what makes the note appear at all.

Speech + fact-check context: primetime White House address 7/16/2026; DHS Sec. Markwayne Mullin
follow-up 7/17. Claims verified against primary sources (agency releases, court opinions, SOS
press releases) wherever possible.

## Note 1 — Noncitizens on the voter rolls

**Trigger tweet (hypothetical):** *"278,000 noncitizens registered to vote. Hundreds of thousands of
dead people too. Our rolls are a disaster. Pass the SAVE Act. 🇺🇸"*

**Why it's the flagship:** it's the speech's most concrete, most-shared number, and it's the one the
user flagged. It's also the claim with the strongest *Republican-official* rebuttal, because
Republican secretaries of state have run these exact audits and published tiny numbers.

**Most convincing counter-context for Republicans:** *government's own track record.* Raw
database/commercial-data matches routinely produce six-figure "noncitizen" estimates that collapse
to dozens once naturalized citizens are removed — and the people who proved this are Republicans.
This beats "experts say it's rare" because it's the GOP's own officials correcting the GOP's own
estimates.

**Gold-standard note:**
> "Noncitizen" counts from database matches routinely collapse on audit. In 2019 Texas's Republican
> secretary of state claimed ~95,000 noncitizen registrations — nearly all were naturalized citizens,
> and he resigned. Iowa's 2,176 estimate fell to 35 who actually voted. Georgia's GOP audit found 20
> of 8.2M. Registered ≠ voted.
> Sources: https://www.sos.texas.gov/about/newsreleases/2019/012519.shtml · https://www.fox5atlanta.com/news/georgias-secretary-state-announces-20-noncitizens-found-be-registered-voters

**Backup sources (Republican-official / conservative / primary):**

- **Georgia — Brad Raffensperger (R):** 2024 audit found **20** noncitizens of **8.2M**; separately, **1,634** who *attempted* to register were caught and blocked. [Fox 5 Atlanta](https://www.fox5atlanta.com/news/georgias-secretary-state-announces-20-noncitizens-found-be-registered-voters) · [GA SOS release, 1,634 blocked](https://sos.ga.gov/news/citizenship-audit-finds-1634-noncitizens-attempted-register-vote) *(sos.ga.gov 403s to bots; loads in browser)*

## Note 2 — China "acquired 220 million voter files / largest compromise in history"

**Trigger tweet:** *"China stole 220 MILLION American voter files — the largest election-data breach
in history. Our elections are compromised."*

**⚠️ Steelman first — this is NOT a fake issue.** The declassified documents (declass. 10 July 2026,
read directly) *do* substantiate large-scale collection: a 2016 file of **204,822,241 U.S. voter
records (45 GB)**, plus state voter databases (North Carolina 8M+ voters, Georgia, Iowa, Kansas, and
more) from **18 states'** 2018 midterm rolls, alongside consumer, **military-personnel**, and medical
databases. A note that waves this away ("just public data, nothing happened") is factually wrong and
will be rated unhelpful. The tweet's real error is the word **"compromised"** applied to *elections*.

**What the documents themselves say the data was for:** every voter-data memo states the *same*
purpose — "mining PII on U.S. citizens," "U.S. identity matching," building a "comprehensive PII
baseline," and **"public opinion analysis on the U.S. General Election."** That is **espionage and
influence-operation targeting**, not vote manipulation. **No document alleges** China accessed a
voting machine, altered a vote, changed a registration, or cast a ballot. (The companion "Summary"
documents are entirely about social-media influence themes — racial tension, immigration, COVID —
confirming the goal was swaying opinion, not counting it.)

**Most convincing counter-context for Republicans:** possessing voter data ≠ compromising the vote.
The registration *content* (names, birth dates, addresses, party, phone, voting history) is
public-record data that states sell to campaigns and brokers — Trump's own speech conceded it was
"bought, stolen, or hacked." That is exactly why Trump's **own** FBI & CISA said in 2020 that
obtaining voter data "did not impact the voting process or the integrity of election results."

**Speech vs. the documents — real discrepancies:**
- **"Largest compromise of election data in history"** → the headline memo is a catalog of *"likely leaked, compromised data"* in which **"most of the entries were targets in other countries"**; only **97 entries** were explicitly U.S.-origin. It's a global PII-aggregation list, not a record-setting *election* breach.
- **"Election security nightmare" / elections "compromised"** → the documents' stated use is PII mining, identity-matching, and public-opinion analysis. Nothing about altering votes, rolls, or machines.
- **"Illicit acquisition"** → in the same speech Trump says the 18-states data was **"bought, stolen, or hacked"** — conceding much was commercially obtained, which the memos echo.
- **Roughly accurate to the docs (don't overclaim these as false):** the **~200M** figure (204.8M-record 2016 file), the **18 states** from the 2018 midterms, and the **2019–2020** timeline all track the documents.

**Gold-standard note:**
> The declassified files describe China *collecting* voter and personal data for espionage and "public
> opinion analysis" — not accessing any voting system, and no document alleges a vote was changed.
> Voter-registration data (name, address, party) is public record that states sell to campaigns.
> Trump's own FBI & CISA (2020): obtaining voter data "did not impact the voting process or the
> integrity of election results."
> Source: ic3.gov/PSA/2020/PSA200928  *(~380 chars — trim toward 280 if submitted)*

**Backup sources:**
- **Trump's own FBI + CISA, 9/28/2020 (GOLD):** "much U.S. voter information can be purchased or acquired through publicly available sources… the acquisition of this data did not impact the voting process or the integrity of election results." [ic3.gov PSA I-091620](https://www.ic3.gov/PSA/2020/PSA200928)
- **The White House documents' own stated purpose** is identity-matching + "public opinion analysis on the U.S. General Election" — influence/espionage, not vote alteration. ([whitehouse.gov/election-integrity](https://www.whitehouse.gov/election-integrity/), "China's Acquisition and Exploitation of American Voter Data"; primary scans read directly. The **~200M** number is corroborated *by these documents*; what they do **not** support is any effect on vote counts, registrations, or results.)
- **Republican-run states publish/sell the same voter file:** Ohio (SOS Frank LaRose, R) posts it free; California sells it for $100. [Ohio voter data portal](https://data.ohiosos.gov/voter) · [NC SBE](https://www.ncsbe.gov/results-data/voter-registration-data)
- **Republican-chaired Senate Intelligence Committee** (Chairman Burr, R-NC; later Rubio, R-FL): even when Russia accessed two states' voter databases in 2016, "no evidence that vote tallying procedures had been compromised" and "no evidence that any voter registration data had been modified or deleted." [Senate Intel Vol. 1](https://www.intelligence.senate.gov/publications/report-select-committee-intelligence-united-states-senate-russian-active-measures) *(verify verbatim against the official PDF; Senate site blocks automated fetch)*

**What changed from the first draft (and why):** the original note claimed this was "just public data for sale," which trivialized a real espionage story and attacked a strawman. The primary documents show genuine large-scale data *collection*, so the honest note concedes the collection and rebuts only the false leap from "China has voter data" to "our elections are compromised" — a leap the documents themselves do not make.

## Note 3 — Voting machines "vulnerable and easily compromised"

**Trigger tweet:** *"Even the government now admits our voting machines are easily hacked. They knew
and covered it up."*

**⚠️ The old rebuttal here was a strawman — rebuilt from the primary docs (read directly).** The claim
rests on two documents Trump declassified: **NICM 2020-003, "Vulnerabilities in US 2020 Election
Infrastructure"** (15 Jan 2020; declassified by DNI Gabbard, released by Trump 3 July 2026), and a
**CIA Note on Venezuela's voting-manipulation capabilities** (declassified by CIA Dir. Ratcliffe). The
decisive move is not to change the subject (Trump's 2024 wins, the Fox settlement) — it's to **quote
the very report Trump waved at the camera**, which says the opposite of his claim.

**What Trump's own document actually says:** he quoted its "adversaries… have the capability to
compromise US election infrastructure" line — but the same NICM states that **vote-tabulation systems
"would be difficult to manipulate on a wide enough scale to alter the election outcome,"** are "not
connected to the Internet or to each other," and that **"postelection audits and paper trails very
likely would uncover such an effort"** (audits required in 38 states). It then warns — presciently —
that adversaries would make **"exaggerated claims… about their ability to affect the election
outcome"** to undermine confidence. Trump quoted the alarming half and dropped the bottom line.

**On the Venezuela "plot":** the CIA Note concludes the IC assessed that neither Smartmatic nor
Venezuela "had the capability… to manipulate the outcome of an election **outside of Venezuela**,"
that Smartmatic **divested its U.S. arm (Sequoia) in 2007**, and that large-scale electronic fraud
even inside Venezuela's 2012 election "did not occur." Smartmatic machines aren't used in U.S.
elections. So the plot proves nothing about U.S. voting.

**Gold-standard note:**
> The intelligence report Trump cites (NICM 2020-003, declassified by his own DNI) says vote-tabulation
> systems "would be difficult to manipulate on a wide enough scale to alter the election outcome," are
> not internet-connected, and that "postelection audits and paper trails very likely would uncover such
> an effort" — audits are required in 38 states. The same report warns adversaries make "exaggerated
> claims… about their ability to affect the election outcome" to undermine confidence.
> Source: https://www.whitehouse.gov/election-integrity/ (doc: "Vulnerabilities in Electronic Voting and Ballot-Counting Systems," NICM 2020-003)

**Speech vs. the documents — real discrepancies:**
- **"Easily compromised" / votes altered undetectably** → the NICM says tabulation is "difficult to manipulate on a wide enough scale to alter the election outcome," and paper trails + audits "very likely would uncover such an effort." The "couldn't be detected even with an audit" line describes a *Venezuela* Devil's-Advocacy hypothetical the CIA labels an "alternative analysis exercise," not a finding — and one requiring the government to control every stage of voting, which it "would not" have outside Venezuela.
- **"People within our government knew and covered it up"** → the government's own Jan 2020 assessment is measured (hard to alter at scale; audits catch it). That is the opposite of a suppressed "machines are easily hacked" finding.
- **Venezuela "plot" → U.S. machines** → the IC found Venezuela/Smartmatic could not manipulate elections outside Venezuela; Smartmatic's U.S. arm was divested in 2007 and its machines aren't used here.

**Backup sources (primary / in-group):**
- **The primary document itself — NICM 2020-003**, declassified by DNI Tulsi Gabbard, approved for public release by President Trump. The strongest possible source for this audience: *it is Trump's own release.* Official hub: [whitehouse.gov/election-integrity](https://www.whitehouse.gov/election-integrity/); the memo is inside the ["Vulnerabilities in Electronic Voting and Ballot-Counting Systems" release](https://www.whitehouse.gov/wp-content/uploads/2026/07/Vulnerabilities-in-Electronic-Voting-and-Ballot-Counting-Systems.zip) (a ZIP — link the hub page in the note itself).
- **CIA Note, "…Venezuela's Electronic Voting Manipulation Capabilities"** (declassified by CIA Dir. John Ratcliffe, 1 July 2026): IC concluded Smartmatic/Venezuela lacked the capability to manipulate elections "outside of Venezuela"; Smartmatic divested Sequoia (its U.S. arm) in 2007. (Same White House release.)
- **Corroboration if a second link is wanted:** Georgia's Republican SOS hand-counted ~5M paper ballots in the 2020 audit and the machine result held. [GA SOS](https://sos.ga.gov/news/historic-first-statewide-audit-paper-ballots-upholds-result-presidential-race) *(sos.ga.gov 403s to bots; loads in a browser)*

**What changed from the first draft (and why):** the original Note 3 changed the subject to Trump's 2024 wins and the Fox–Dominion settlement — tangential to the *specific* declassified claim and easy to wave off ("hackable ≠ hacked in 2024"). Reading the primary document showed a far stronger, non-strawman rebuttal: the report Trump cited concludes vote counts are hard to alter at scale and audits would catch it, and it explicitly predicts the "exaggerated claim" tactic. Quoting Trump's own declassified document is maximally persuasive here.
