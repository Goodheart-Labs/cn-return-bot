The two evals

The dashed frames in the diagram show which part each eval tests. Eval 1 gives the extractor one chunk of a post and asks whether the right claim comes out. Eval 2 gives the whole claim check one claim and asks whether it ends in the right decision, a note or no note. The gate, the image descriptions and the rating are not tested by either.
