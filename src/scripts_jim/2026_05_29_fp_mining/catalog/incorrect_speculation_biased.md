# Reject examples: Incorrect, Opinion/Speculation, Argumentative/Biased

These notes were rejected by the X crowd because their **content** is defective — not because the underlying tweet was fine. Each falls into one of three failure modes: (1) the note asserts a factual claim that is itself wrong; (2) the note states speculation, inference, or opinion as though it were established fact; (3) the note frames a contested claim in a one-sided or politically charged way. The general lesson: a note that introduces a new falsehood, or that substitutes the writer's inference for verifiable sourced facts, is worse than no note at all — it trains raters to distrust the system and gets penalized by the crowd regardless of whether the original tweet was genuinely misleading.

### Wrong attribution replacing a wrong attribution

**Tweet:** Video posted as "Erika Kirk's entrance at the TPUSA All-American Halftime Show tonight" (7.6M views).
**Note (REJECT):** "The video in this post is from Britney Spears' performance at the 2002 Macy's 4th of July event, not Erika Kirk's entrance at the TPUSA All-American Halftime Show. Kirk was not present at the show."
**Why reject:** Reject notes that confidently name the true source of misattributed content when that identification is itself wrong. The note correctly spots the misattribution but substitutes a second false identity (Britney Spears, 2002) for the actual footage (Charlotte Flair, WrestleMania 33, 2017) — so it adds a new factual error rather than correcting the original one.

**Note (REJECT):** "Eastern Screech Owls can exhibit erythrism, a documented genetic mutation causing reddish-orange feathers. The second image in this post is digitally altered, and no new owl species has been discovered or announced by wildlife authorities."

Erythrism in Eastern Screech Owls is real, but the bird in question is a snowy owl — a different species with a different, documented explanation (likely pollution/contamination). The unsupported "digitally altered" assertion adds a second unverified claim.

**Tweet:** Kim Dotcom asserts "Trump raped children with Jeffrey Epstein" as an established fact.
